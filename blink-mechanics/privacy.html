Privacy Policy

Blink Mechanics Uploader · last updated 24 October 2026

Who uses this app

Blink Mechanics Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner, Will Covello, to manage the Blink Mechanics YouTube channel. It is not available to the public.

What Google data it accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload and schedule videos on the Blink Mechanics channel.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to confirm it is connected to the correct channel and to read back that channel's own uploads, schedules and public statistics.

It does not access email, contacts, files, other channels or any other Google account data.

How data is stored

The authorization token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and is used only by the owner's automated workflow. Channel statistics and upload records are kept in that private repository.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/WCovello/WCovello.github.io/issues.
